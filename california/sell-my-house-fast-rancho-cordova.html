Rancho Cordova

Sell Your Rancho Cordova House Fast for Cash

Rancho Cordova has two kinds of housing and we buy both. The older part of town between Folsom Boulevard and Highway 50 was built in the 1950s and 1960s for Aerojet and Mather Field workers: modest single-story homes on big lots, many of them rentals now. South of the highway, Anatolia, Capital Village and the newer neighborhoods near Sunrise Boulevard are 1990s and 2000s stucco two-stories with HOAs and Mello-Roos taxes. Whether yours is a 1958 ranch with a sagging roof or a 2005 house you cannot carry anymore, Redwood Home Buyers will make a written cash offer within 24 hours.

Rancho Cordova has a large renter population for a suburb, and many of the older houses have been rentals for twenty or thirty years. Those are the houses that come to us: tenant turnover, deferred repairs, a landlord who lives in another state and is finished. We also hear from Highway 50 commuters relocating for work, from heirs, and from owners who have fallen behind. In every case the answer is the same: one visit, a written offer, a closing date you choose, and no repairs, fees or commissions.

Where We Buy

Where We Buy in Rancho Cordova

Rancho Cordova is really two markets. Older homes north of Highway 50 often go to investors who plan to renovate, while the newer Anatolia and Capital Village homes sell mostly to financed families. If your house is one of the older ones and needs work, you are already competing for the same investor buyers who call us, except they will ask you to pay an agent and wait. We make the offer directly.

Houses with dated interiors are the first to sit when the county market slows. Rancho Cordova also has light rail stations along Folsom Boulevard that keep investor interest steady, and Mello-Roos districts in some of the newer tracts that buyers ask about. We know the area and price each house against its own neighborhood, not a citywide number.
